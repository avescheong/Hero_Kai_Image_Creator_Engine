## 1. Executive Architecture: Translating Pythonia into a *Capybara Go!* Chibi Roguelite Auto-Battler Engine

To adapt all 9 canonical Pythonia characters into a **Capybara Go! (Habby)**–style vertical portrait roguelite text-adventure and auto-battler RPG without losing a single defining character detail, this pipeline introduces the **`capybara_go_chibi` Graphics Adapter** governed by [`01_character_genomes`](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1sQZ8BW_8RZcP76uJbnS46z0hClfXoKGd):

1. **Squishy "Mochi-Bean" Chibi Proportions (`2.0` to `2.3 Heads Tall`)**:
   - **Human Heroes & Masters (`2.2 Heads Tall`)**: Oversized rounded head (`~45%` of total character height) with soft chubby cheeks, glossy pill-shaped eyes with big white catchlights, compact bean-shaped torso, and stubby bouncy limbs that perform a signature **squash-and-stretch waddle-march** during `Day 1 -> Day 60` auto-progression.
   - **Pet Familiars & Hatchlings (`1.7` to `1.9 Heads Tall`)**: String (`string`), the Baby Praying Mantis Spell Guardian (`spell_guardian`), and the 4 Shapeshift Spirits (`str`, `int`, `float`, `bool`) adopt ultra-round, huggable **plush-mochi silhouettes** that trot or hover right behind Hero Kai in a single-file party train.
2. **Bold Vector-Gouache Sticker Linework & Shading (`#34221A` Outer Contour)**:
   - **Dual-Weight Outlines**: Thick, smooth, rounded dark cocoa-espresso outer contour (`#34221A`, `5px–6px` equivalent) that makes every character pop like a die-cut vinyl sticker against scrolling biome backdrops, paired with clean `2px` interior detail strokes.
   - **Soft Cel + Cream Highlight Shading**: Warm pastel-meets-vibrant gouache fills with glossy pill-shaped rim highlights on helmets, pauldrons, potion glass, and eyes.
3. **1.6× Iconographic Micro-Detail Magnification (Zero-Loss Insignia Rule)**:
   - In `2.2-head` chibi proportions, tiny realistic buckles disappear. Therefore, the `capybara_go_chibi` adapter **magnifies every canonical insignia by `1.6×–1.8×`** into bold toy-like focal charms:
     - **Hero Kai's Golden Crescent Brooch (`#D4AF37`)**: Enlarged into a chunky, gleaming golden crescent clasp at his throat.
     - **Hero Kai's Tzu Chi Lotus & Dharma Ship Belt Medallion (`#1F6A38` / `#C29B38`)**: Enlarged into a prominent round emerald-and-gold center belt badge.
     - **Hero Kai's Asymmetric Hip Gear**: Right hip carries 1 plump rounded brown satchel; left hip carries **3 chunky round corked glass potion bulbs** (emerald `#2ECC71`, ruby `#E74C3C`, sapphire `#3A86FF`).
4. **Capybara Go! `9:16` Split-Screen Viewport & Facing Rules**:
   - **Top 45% Auto-Battle & Travel Stage**:
     - **Left Party Side (Facing Right `3/4` Profile)**: Hero Kai + up to 2 active Pets (String & Baby Mantis / Shapeshift Spirit) + optional Mount always face **Right (`->`)** as they auto-march across days and battle enemies.
     - **Right Encounter Side (Facing Left `3/4` Profile)**: All Event NPCs (Grandma Looma, Ch. 3 Blacksmith, Ch. 9 Master Grimsby, Great Wizard) and all Bosses/Minions appear on the right side of the stage facing **Left (`<-`)** so they naturally converse or clash with Hero Kai!
   - **Bottom 55% Text-Adventure Log, 3-Card Skill Draft & 6-Slot Gear UI**:
     - Requires dedicated `4×4` icon sheets for **Roguelite Skill Draft Cards** (Python spells + weapon passives across 5 rarity tiers), **6-Slot Equipment Loadout Badges**, and **Scrolling Day-by-Day Event Node Props**.
