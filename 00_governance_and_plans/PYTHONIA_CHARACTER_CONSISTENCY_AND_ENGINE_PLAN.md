## 1. Executive Purpose & Cross-Graphics Consistency Architecture

This document serves as the **Single Source of Visual & Technical Truth (SSOT)** for building a deterministic **Character Consistency & Multi-Graphics Generation Engine** inside [Kai Asset For RPG](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1ampGrS0JM8joJjLeULudV8tKjfgFzzO2). Its objective is to guarantee **zero anatomical drift, zero missing insignia, zero left/right equipment swaps, and zero style fracture** when generating or rendering any Pythonia character across every visual medium:

1. **Widescreen Storybook Illustrations (`16:9`, `1920x1080` / `2560x1440`)**: Direct integrated scenes and Track A 5-layer parallax stages ([Story_Website](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1y56ALXDfFmEL8FztXiMuLmyNA3xmCMcc) and [ENVIRONMENT_LIBRARY_MASTER_BLUEPRINT.md](https://drive.google.com/file/d/17XgKnlNLczA1p4-fZYWb_GpQPcPsFIWy/view?usp=drivesdk)).
2. **2D / 128-Bit HD-2D Side-View Action & Platformer Sprite Sheets (`4x4` and `6x3` Grids, `1024x1024`)**: Modular paper-doll armor/weapon overlays and MapleStory-style locomotion/combat sheets ([Kai_Game_Design_Project AGENT.md](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1Bz3cNypAfOnSztgfHtLRO_3BGQcly4ndtMQ5Q5oRwcg/edit?usp=drivesdk&ouid=105403315594343730003)).
3. **SRPG Tactical Grid Units (`12x8` Battlefield Grid, `4x4` Directional Sheets)**: High-readability isometric/top-down tactical sprites across all 13 zones ([ASSET_INVENTORY_INDEX.md](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1KPgtUoWHDBM0uYL-kTlZ0cjeIXbjUdo4iCYNn9YmnSI/edit?usp=drivesdk&ouid=105403315594343730003)).
4. **16-Bit Top-Down ZEP Overworld & Sanctuary Sprites (`960x540` Logical / `1920x1080` Retina)**: Multi-tier zoom exploration sprites (`1.00x` World, `1.35x` Region, `1.55x` Exterior, `1.85x` Sanctuary).
5. **RPG Dialogue Bust Portraits & Lip-Sync Phoneme Atlases (`1:1`, `512x512`)**: 8-emotion dialogue busts + 8 Preston Blair mouth phoneme shapes.
6. **Orthographic 4-View Model Sheets & Equipment Callouts (`16:9`)**: Symmetrical `Front`, `3/4`, `Side`, and `Back` turnarounds on a locked baseline.

### 1.1 Universal Art Style DNA & Global Negative Contract

Every generation call across the engine prepends the locked style DNA from [presets.py](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1VTFn1mS2WUdxcf9kH2it44Gv3I5GLjlM/view?usp=drivesdk) and [scene_generation_manifest.json](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1Wg9my6lFXQwx17lDIpTldGehDO-I-PYp/view?usp=drivesdk):

* **Primary Aesthetic**: Studio Ghibli meets Akira Toriyama (*Dragon Quest*) hand-drawn children's fantasy storybook & 2D RPG aesthetic.
* **Linework Contract**: Clean warm-sepia and dark-charcoal ink contours (`#3E2B23` to `#4A3728`) with organic hand-drawn lineweight variation and crisp silhouette separation.
* **Shading & Surface Contract**: Soft watercolor washes and painterly cel-shaded gouache over subtle antique cream cotton paper grain (`#FAF4E8` for storybook spreads; pure flat `#FFFFFF` prior to border-seeded BFS alpha extraction for game sprites).
* **Global Negative Prompt Lock**: `photorealistic, 3D render, CGI, octane render, unreal engine, modern clothing (except PRO_S01), jeans, sneakers (in fantasy realms), sunglasses, cyberpunk, grimdark, horror, bloody, gore, grotesque, distorted anatomy, extra limbs, extra fingers, missing fingers, fused fingers, blurry, low resolution, bad hands, deformed face, plastic skin, horizontal dashed line, screenshot artifacts, crop marks, watermark, text, signature, Ember pet, meat/non-vegetarian food`.

### 3.3 Paper-Doll & Action Animation Parity Rules (`Kai_Game_Design_Project` + `Kai_RPG`)

To prevent the animation mismatches identified in earlier game builds (where climbing a ladder or swinging a weapon snapped Kai back to a default outfit or auto-fired continuously), the engine enforces **Strict Row-by-Row Paper-Doll & State Parity**:

1. **Independent Armor × Weapon Matrix**:
   * **5 Equippable Armor Tiers**: `blue_hoodie` / `sage_hoodie` (Starter M1), `teal_tunic` (Scout), `moss_cloak` (Standard Adventure Outfit A), `steel_plate` (Master Grimsby's Dwarven Steel Armor), and `lotus_cape` (Mastery Tzu Chi Lotus Mantle).
   * **4 Equippable Weapon Tiers**: `wooden_sword` (Beginner), `iron_sword` (Fused `IronSword`), `forge_hammer` (Dwarven Rune Hammer), and `twin_blades` / `spellblade` (Wizard's Blade).
   * Every locomotion and special action sheet (`idle`, `walk`, `run_front` with `Shift+D`, `jump`, `jump_front` with `D+Space`, `crouch`, `crawl_front` with `S+D`, `climb_ladder` with `W`, `melee_slash` with `P`/`X`, `cast_spell` with `E`, `shield_guard` with Sun Aegis Shield, and `interact` with `F`) must be generated across **all armor variants** so climbing a ladder or casting a spell never reverts Kai's equipped armor.
2. **7-Gate ACVE Post-Processing & Verification (`acve_sprite_tool.py` & [asset_processor.py](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1FaLOf5E3VpalnYxc4Y5alIEZWD2xl7IP/view?usp=drivesdk))**:
   * **Gate 1 (Format & RGBA)**: Valid 4-channel `RGBA` PNG/WebP.
   * **Gate 2 (Dimension & Grid Parity)**: Exact `1024x1024` (`4x4` of `256x256`), `1536x768` (`6x3` of `256x256`), or `512x512` with zero odd-pixel drift.
   * **Gate 3 (Border-Seeded BFS Alpha Flood-Fill)**: Removes outer `#FFFFFF` or checkerboard backdrops from the 4 image borders inward (`white_threshold=238`) while **preserving 100% of interior white/cream pixels** (Kai's `#F4EFE6` cream tunic, Great Wizard's `#F2F4F7` snow-white beard, Blacksmith's `#C8CDD2` triple-braided beard, String's `#F5E6CC` cream muzzle, and eye catchlights).
   * **Gate 4 (Cell Occupancy & Zero Gutter Bleed)**: Confirms every cell in an `R x C` grid contains non-empty sprite pixels and zero weapon/cloak bleed across cell borders.
   * **Gate 5 (Bottom-Center `(0.5, 1.0)` Pivot Normalization)**: Aligns every ground-contact frame to a uniform baseline so walk, sprint, and attack animations never jitter vertically.
   * **Gate 6 (Color Swatch & Micro-Insignia Audit)**: Programmatically checks that each generated sprite contains the character's mandatory hex clusters (e.g., `#345830` cloak + `#D4AF37` gold clasp + `#1F6A38` Tzu Chi buckle + `#2ECC71`/`#E74C3C`/`#3A86FF` potions for Kai).
   * **Gate 7 (Aseprite JSON Atlas & SHA-256 Seal)**: Emits a companion `.json` descriptor containing frame rectangles, `(0.5, 1.0)` pivots, animation tags, and SHA-256 checksums.
