## 1. Executive Architecture: Translating Pythonia into a Final Fantasy Tactics (FFT) Isometric Engine

To generate **Final Fantasy Tactics (Ivalice / War of the Lions)**–grade tactical RPG visuals across all 9 canonical Pythonia character genomes without losing micro-details (belt buckles, clasps, asymmetric hip gear, or beard/helmet disambiguation), the engine separates every character into **Two Synchronized FFT Visual Layers** governed by [`01_character_genomes`](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1sQZ8BW_8RZcP76uJbnS46z0hClfXoKGd):

1. **Layer A — Akihiko Yoshida–Inspired Tactical Job Art & Vellum Status Portraits (`FFT-YOSHIDA-ART`)**:
   - **Linework & Medium**: Expressive tapered dark sepia-charcoal ink lines (`#3B271E`) with delicate cross-hatching along cloth folds, pauldrons, and boots, painted over warm aged vellum/parchment wash tones (`#F3E8D2`) with rich gouache-and-tempera shadows.
   - **Proportions**: `4.0` to `4.5 heads tall` tactical standing job figures with bold silhouette readability, oversized layered cloaks, heavy leather straps, sturdy cuffed boots, and expressive eyes with crisp white catchlights.
   - **Use Cases**: Status screen full-body Job Class cards, bottom-screen tactical dialogue portraits, and diagonal Boss/Spell Cut-In banners.
2. **Layer B — True 2:1 Dimetric Isometric Battlefield Unit Sprites (`FFT-ISOMETRIC-4x4`)**:
   - **Isometric Camera Lock**: Strictly locked **45° horizontal yaw + ~30° overhead elevation pitch (`2:1` dimetric diamond tile ratio, `26.565°` tile slope)** so every unit stands naturally on a 3D stepped-elevation diorama board (`12×8` or `16×16` isometric grid).
   - **4-Diagonal Facing System (`SE`, `SW`, `NW`, `NE`)**: Unlike flat side-scrollers, FFT battlefield units always face one of the four isometric diagonals:
     - **South-East (`SE` — Front-Right)**: Primary tactical showcase angle (displays face, chest brooch/buckle, and right-hip satchel).
     - **South-West (`SW` — Front-Left)**: Secondary front angle (displays face, main-hand weapon, and left-hip 3-potion holster).
     - **North-West (`NW` — Back-Left)**: Rear-left diagonal (displays cloak back, diagonal back-scabbard weapon, and hair/helmet back).
     - **North-East (`NE` — Back-Right)**: Rear-right diagonal (displays back-right silhouette and off-hand shield/prop).
   - **Compact Tactical Proportions (`3.0` to `3.3 Heads Tall`) & Micro-Detail Scaling**:
     - Head occupies `~30–33%` of unit height; hands, boots, and weapons are scaled `1.35×–1.40×` so sword blades, forging hammers, weaving shuttles, and spellbooks read unmistakably at tactical camera zoom.
     - All micro-insignias (such as Kai's `#D4AF37` crescent clasp and `#1F6A38` Tzu Chi lotus buckle) are simplified into crisp 3-to-5-pixel high-contrast iconographic clusters rather than muddy noise.

## 3. Standard FFT 16-Cell (`4×4`) Isometric Unit Sheet Blueprint

Every character and boss in Batches `FFT_B01` through `FFT_B06` follows a standardized **Final Fantasy Tactics 16-Cell (`4×4`) Isometric Sprite Sheet Topology** so your HTML5/Canvas engine can swap any unit into the tactical state machine with zero custom offsets:

* **Type I Sheet — 4-Direction Isometric Locomotion, March-in-Place & Elevation Jump (`4×4` = 16 Cells)**:
  * **Row 1 (`SE` Front-Right Facing — Cells 0–3)**: `(0) SE Idle March Frame 1`, `(1) SE Idle March Frame 2 (Arms/Cloak Swing)`, `(2) SE Walk Stride`, `(3) SE High-Elevation Tile Leap`.
  * **Row 2 (`SW` Front-Left Facing — Cells 4–7)**: `(4) SW Idle March Frame 1`, `(5) SW Idle March Frame 2`, `(6) SW Walk Stride`, `(7) SW High-Elevation Tile Leap`.
  * **Row 3 (`NW` Back-Left Facing — Cells 8–11)**: `(8) NW Idle March Frame 1`, `(9) NW Idle March Frame 2`, `(10) NW Walk Stride`, `(11) NW High-Elevation Tile Leap`.
  * **Row 4 (`NE` Back-Right Facing — Cells 12–15)**: `(12) NE Idle March Frame 1`, `(13) NE Idle March Frame 2`, `(14) NE Walk Stride`, `(15) NE High-Elevation Tile Leap`.
* **Type II Sheet — Isometric Combat, Python Spell Chant, Critical Kneel & KO (`4×4` = 16 Cells)**:
  * **Row 1 (`SE` Primary Melee / Weapon Skill — Cells 0–3)**: `(0) SE Weapon Wind-Up`, `(1) SE Primary Slash / Hammer Strike`, `(2) SE Signature Swordskill / Rune Impact`, `(3) SE Shield Block / Guard Crouch`.
  * **Row 2 (`SW` Secondary Melee & Item Use — Cells 4–7)**: `(4) SW Weapon Wind-Up`, `(5) SW Primary Slash / Strike`, `(6) SW Item / Potion Throw`, `(7) SW Sidestep Evasion`.
