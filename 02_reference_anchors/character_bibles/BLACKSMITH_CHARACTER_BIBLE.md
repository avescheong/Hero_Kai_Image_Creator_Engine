*Ch. 3 Iron Outpost (`ENV_R03` — Spell 3: `FUSE`) — Distinct from Ch. 9 Master Grimsby (`ENV_R08`)*

---

## 1. Core Character Identity
- **Name**: **Blacksmith** *(Iron Outpost Blacksmith — `ENV_R03`)*
- **Role / Title**: Village Master Forgeman & Armorer of the Iron Outpost (welds Kai's shattered sword halves with `+` `FUSE`)
- **Age & Stature**: ~55–68 years old, **~150 cm tall** (`~4.5 heads` tall — stocky, ultra-broad barrel-chested frame; ~8cm taller than Kai, ~6cm shorter than Grandma Looma).
- **Critical Cast Distinction**:
  - **Ch. 3 Iron Outpost Blacksmith (`ENV_R03`)** = horned riveted iron helmet + **silver-white triple-braided beard** (this character).
  - **Ch. 9 Deep Dwarven Forge Master Grimsby (`ENV_R08`)** = bare brow with brass forge goggles + **fiery copper-red braided beard** (see `MASTER_GRIMSBY_CHARACTER_BIBLE.md`).

## 2. Head, Helmet, Hair & Triple-Braided Beard Invariants
- **Helmet**: Domed riveted steel/iron skullcap (`#6E7885`) with central vertical iron strap, riveted brow rim, and **two short upward-curving ivory/cream horns** (`#DDD2BE`) anchored in metal cuffs.
- **Hair & Brows**: Flowing silver-grey locks out the back of the helmet, thick bushy silver eyebrows.
- **Face**: Square rugged ruddy-tanned craftsman face (`#D99B78`), alert dark eyes, broad ruddy nose.
- **Triple-Braided Beard**: Huge silver-white beard split neatly into **three braids** (one thick long center braid + two shorter side braids secured with brown cuffs) beneath a sweeping walrus mustache.

---

## 3. Canonical Attire & Forge Gear
1. **Sleeveless Frayed Olive-Slate Tunic (`#58635A`)**: Rough-spun frayed armholes exposing massive scarred biceps and shoulders.
2. **Heavy Soot-Stained Saddle-Brown Leather Apron (`#8A5A36`)**: Thick chest bib with upper tool pocket, wide shoulder straps, lower soot/ash wear, and small hip leather pouch.
3. **Cuffed Leather Gauntlets (`#6E482C`)** & **Olive Trousers (`#666852`)** tucked into dark brown folded-cuff boots (`#5C3B24`).

## 4. Signature Forge Tools & Workshop Props
- **Steel Forging Hammer**: Heavy rectangular iron hammerhead (`#6D747E`) on sturdy hardwood shaft.
- **Iron Forging Tongs & Glowing Steel Blank**: Articulated tongs clamping white-hot orange steel (`#FF7A29`) over a horn-tipped **anvil on an oak stump**.
- **Masterwork Swords & Forge Pup**: Finished arming swords racked on timber walls + sleeping golden-tan terrier pup (`String`'s visual anchor cameo) on burlap sacks.
