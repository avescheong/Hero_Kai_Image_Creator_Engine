## 1. Core Character Identity
- **Name**: **Grandma Looma** *(Canonical v3.0 spelling; Loom Forest `ENV_R02`, Spell 2: `WEAVE` — `print("Hello", "Pythonia!")`)*
- **Role / Title**: Master Weaver & Kind-Hearted Village Elder of Loom Forest, Pythonia
- **Age & Stature**: ~62–70 years old, **~156 cm tall** (`~4.9 heads` tall — noticeably taller than Hero Kai at `~142 cm`, taller than Iron Outpost Blacksmith at `~150 cm`, reaching Great Wizard's shoulder height at `~180 cm`).
- **Personality & Vibe**: Nurturing, perceptive, joyful artisan who teaches Kai how the comma `,` weaves words together with breathing room just like threads on her giant oak loom. First introduces the unnamed golden-tan pup (**String**) who follows Kai onward from her cottage.

---

## 2. Head, Hair & Facial Invariants
- **Hair**: Warm dusty **ash-silver / blonde-grey** (`#A89F8D`) soft wavy hair parted near center and gathered neatly into a **low coiled bun at the nape of her neck**.
- **Face**: Round, warm fair peach skin with soft rosy cheeks, gentle forehead expression lines, crow's feet smile wrinkles, and a **cozy rounded nose**.
- **Eyes**: Bright expressive **sky/slate-blue eyes** (`#4F7FA8`) with warm arched grey-blonde eyebrows.
- **Earrings**: Small round **copper-brown wooden stud earrings** (`#8E5B3E`).

## 3. Canonical Attire & Visual Anchors
1. **Ochre Botanical Leaf Blouse (`#C9965B`)**:
   - Long sleeves patterned all over with folk leaf/petal motifs in terracotta red (`#9B4A38`), sage green (`#5A7A58`), and slate teal (`#4C6B6E`).
   - **V-notch neckline trimmed in an embroidered sage-green zigzag geometric border**.
2. **Olive-Brown Vest & Skirt (`#7B6B4F`)**:
   - Sleeveless earthy olive-brown bodice over a matching ankle-length gathered skirt.
3. **Cream Linen Bib Apron (`#EDE4CE`)**:
   - Clean off-white bib apron worn on front, tying in a neat fabric bow at the back waist.
4. **Signature Neck-Cord Reading Glasses**:
   - Small round wire-rimmed spectacles (`#555A5E`) hanging on a **thin tan leather cord around her neck** over the apron chest (or perched comfortably on her nose when inspecting fine weave drafts).
5. **Footwear**:
   - Sturdy dark brown buckled/buttoned village leather workshop shoes (`#4E3728`).
