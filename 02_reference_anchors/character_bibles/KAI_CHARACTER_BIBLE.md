## 1. Character Overview & Concept

**Kai** is the young protagonist of *Pythonia*, a fantasy adventure series blending courageous exploration with compassionate service, healing, and mindfulness inspired by the Tzu Chi ethos.

- **Age**: 11–12 years old
- **Archetype**: Junior Paladin / Alchemist Scout / Young Humanitarian Adventurer
- **Proportions**: 4.2 to 4.5 heads tall (classic heroic anime/manga proportion, reminiscent of *Studio Ghibli*, *Dragon Quest*, and *Akira Toriyama*)
- **Core Values**: Courage, Compassion, Curiosity, Environmental stewardship, Healing

## 5. Art Direction & Style Guide for Storybooks

To ensure 100% visual consistency across all storybook pages:

- **Art Style Engine**: Studio Ghibli meets Akira Toriyama (Dragon Quest) fantasy illustration.
- **Line Art**: Clean, sensitive warm-sepia or dark-charcoal ink outlines with dynamic hand-drawn lineweight.
- **Coloring**: Soft painterly gouache and watercolor textures, warm natural lighting, gentle paper grain.
- **Composition for Storybooks**:
  - Always leave **text-safe copy space** (open sky, soft foliage, misty gradient) for storybook typography.
  - Maintain consistent scale between Kai and companion creatures/characters.
  - Avoid photorealism, CGI sheen, or hyper-complex rendering that detracts from storybook warmth.
