## 1. Core Character Identity
- **Name**: **Master Grimsby**
- **Canonical Zone & Spell**: Chapter 9 (`ENV_R08` — **Deep Dwarven Forge of Master Grimsby**, Spell 9: `FORGE` — `hp = 100 - 20`, `+`, `-`, `*`, `/`)
- **Role / Title**: Master of the Deep Dwarven Forge; tests Kai's grit through humble hard work (hauling slag, shoveling coal, scrubbing soot) before unlocking gleaming Dwarven Steel Armor and a finely balanced Wizard's Blade.
- **Age & Stature**: ~65 years old, **~142 cm tall** (`~4.4 heads` tall — stout, rugged subterranean dwarf proportions matching Kai's shoulder height, ultra-broad cinder-weathered chest and forearms).
- **Critical Cast Distinction**:
  - **Ch. 3 Iron Outpost Blacksmith (`ENV_R03`)** = horned riveted helmet + **silver-white triple-braided beard**.
  - **Ch. 9 Master Grimsby (`ENV_R08`)** = **NO horned helmet** (bare brow with brass forge goggles pushed onto forehead) + **roaring fiery copper-red & burnt-orange braided beard**.

## 2. Head, Hair & Fiery-Beard Invariants
- **Headwear**: **NO helmet** — wears adjustable **brass-rimmed ember-tinted forge goggles** pushed up onto a leather strap across his broad forehead.
- **Hair & Brows**: Thick bushy **auburn-copper hair** swept back under the goggle strap; heavy expressive **fiery copper eyebrows** with ember glint.
- **Face**: Broad weathered dwarf craftsman face speckled lightly with forge soot, expressive warm amber-brown eyes (`#7A421D`), bulbous kindly smith's nose, booming grin when Kai proves his grit.
- **Fiery Beard & Mustache**: Roaring **fiery copper-red & burnt-orange (`#C94A29` → `#E07A38`)** wide braided beard bound with **small tarnished brass cuffs** under a thick sweeping copper mustache.
