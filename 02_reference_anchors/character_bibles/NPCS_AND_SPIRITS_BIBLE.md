## 1. The Four Shapeshift Spirits (Spell 7 & Spell 8: `ENV_R06` / `ENV_R07`)
1. **`str` Scroll Spirit (`#F6C863` Golden Parchment)**: Floating rolled golden ribbon-scroll spirit with bright friendly ink eyes and unfurling silk tails; upgrades **String**'s telepathic speech bond.
2. **`int` Stone Golem (`#7E7872` Carved Mossy Granite)**: Stout chunky rune-carved boulder helper capable of crisp push-ups and log lifting (Whole Numbers Only).
3. **`float` Water Droplet (`#4EA8DE` Luminous Azure)**: Smooth translucent floating sky-blue droplet companion glistening with tiny decimals like dewdrops; nourishes dry crops.
4. **`bool` Truth Gem (`#2ECC71` Emerald / `#E74C3C` Crimson)**: Octagonal hovering crystal spirit flashing emerald `TRUE` or crimson `FALSE` depending on honesty and logic.

- **Hooded Signpost-Keeper (`ENV_R10` · The Final Crossroads)**: Lantern-bearingcloaked elder keeper standing quietly by the three wooden signposts (`DRAGON`, `TREASURE`, `HOME`) and golden colon (`:`) gates.
