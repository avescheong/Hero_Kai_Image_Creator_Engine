## 1. Core Character Identity
- **Name**: **Baby Praying Mantis (True Spell Guardian)**

- **Chitin & Carapace**: Fresh dew-lit **spring leaf-green & jade chitin** (`#48C984` to `#2A9D65`) — bright, translucent-soft, and welcoming rather than jagged dark iron.
- **Eyes**: Big round expressive **emerald crystal eyes (`#2ECC71`)** with joyful sparkling white highlights.
- **Head & Antennae**: Small rounded mantis head with delicate curling leaf-tipped antennae (`#8CF0BD`) and zero jagged crown spikes.
- **Limbs & Wings**: Delicate folded front raptorial forelimbs clasped politely like tiny hands, tiny translucent golden-emerald fairy-like wing buds (`#F6C863` shimmer), and a gentle floating golden dawn halo rune behind wings.
