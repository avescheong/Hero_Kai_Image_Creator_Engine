## 1. Character Overview & Concept

**The Great Wizard** *(Canonical Story v3.0)* is the wise, kind-hearted archmage and mentor figure in the *Pythonia* universe—guiding **Hero Kai** from his Prologue Road arrival (`ENV_R00`) through the Cursed Riverbank (`ENV_R05` — gifting the **Spatial Dimensional Pocket** pouch) to the **Silver-Leafed Tree Emerald Farewell (`ENV_R05A`)**. Visually, he shares the exact hand-drawn ink (`#3E2B23`) and soft watercolor storybook aesthetic (`#FAF4E8`) as Hero Kai across both **illustrated storybooks** and **2D/2.5D game assets**.

- **Apparent Age**: 65–75 years old
- **Archetype**: Grand Archmage / Benevolent Mentor / Keeper of Foundations
- **Proportions**: ~180 cm (`5.5` to `6.2 heads` tall, ~195 cm with tall pointed hat); dignified, approachable sage posture
- **Core Personality**: Wise, gentle, grandfatherly, whimsical, protective

## 3. Costuming & Signature Equipment Breakdown

### A. Grand Archmage Celestial Robes (Canonical Default)
1. **The Celestial Pointed Hat**: Deep indigo / royal slate-blue (`#4B679E`) with curved tip, rose-plum (`#7C4B7D`) chevron hatband, embroidered gold stars and crescent moon (`#D4AF37`).
2. **Purple Shoulder Mantle**: Deep royal purple (`#5A3C7E`) bordered in mauve (`#9E688F`) golden scrollwork with a gold crescent moon on right shoulder.
3. **Twilight-Blue Outer Over-Robe**: Open front twilight slate-blue (`#4A6B9C`) robe with purple lining, bell sleeves, mauve wave-scroll borders, and celestial lower hem filigree.
4. **Lavender Inner Tunic**: Soft lavender-periwinkle (`#7A74A8`) with golden diamond chest sigil.
5. **Braided Cord Belt & Tassels**: Braided golden-brown cord rope (`#9C7A42`) with twin hanging golden tassels at right hip.
6. **Arcane Crystal Staff**: Tall rustic hardwood staff twisting into a double-helix spiral wood cradle (`#7E5A38`) clasping a glowing faceted **cyan-ice blue mana crystal** (`#42D4FF` to `#8CF0FF`).
7. **Canon Story Prop (`ENV_R05`)**: Shimmering silver **Spatial Dimensional Pocket** pouch gifted to Kai.
